What is computed dynamically?

The application dynamically computes unique record counts, text coverage, activity grouping, route/review counts, filter results, import revision handling, manual overrides and export manifests. Conversation summaries and evidence-based initial route suggestions are pre-generated review aids. They are not live AI output. A source revision that changes the transcript marks the analysis pending review rather than silently reusing old conclusions.
