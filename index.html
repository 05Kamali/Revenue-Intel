Metric Definitions & Denominator Mathematical Audit

        
Strict compliance with candidate assignment page 1

      

      
    

    

      

        

          
1. Total Selected Records (44)

          

            What was counted: Count of unique (source_type, source_id) pairs represented in the current workspace.

            Period: Exactly one local calendar date: Friday, 21 August 2026 (Asia/Kolkata).

            Composition: 30 phone calls (20 background + 10 with text) and 14 calendar meetings (6 without text + 8 with text).

            Absence / Boundary impact: This is a deliberately selected assessment sample, not a complete or representative sample of team performance. Do not infer historical connection rates.
          

        


        

          
2. Usable Supplied Transcript Coverage (17 → 18 / 44 = 40.9%)

          

            Denominator: All 44 selected source records.

            Numerator: Records where payload.transcript (calls) or transcript_payload.turns (meetings) contains non-empty text (17 in batchInitial; 18 post-update).

            Absence / Boundary impact: Missing text is an assessment delivery condition (redacted/withheld for test), not proof that the call or meeting did not happen.
          

        


        

          
3. Routing & Review Queue Counts

          

            Logic: Multi-destination routing. One conversation may serve multiple business purposes (e.g. M07 routes to both Customer Support and Internal Follow-up; C03 routes to both Deal Follow-up and Sales Coaching).

            Denominator note: Route counts are not mutually exclusive percentages of 44.
          

        


        

          
4. Activity by Person & Role

          

            Calls: Grouped by rep_name (Arun, Leela, Meera, Kiran).

            Meetings: Grouped by organizer_email.

            Critical Boundary: A meeting organiser is NOT automatically the salesperson, attendee, or deal owner.
